WHY THIS EXISTS

Government pages tell you the rule. StillWaiting turns the rule into a date.

Published service standards are not always legal deadlines or guarantees. Some requests can take longer, and extensions or special circumstances may apply. StillWaiting gives you a plain-language estimate and sends you back to the official source for the final word.
